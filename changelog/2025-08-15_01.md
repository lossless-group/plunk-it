## Why Care?

If you draft long-form content in Obsidian — newsletter issues, founder updates, company-wide notes — there's a stubborn last-mile problem: the draft is in markdown, but the destination is Plunk (or another transactional email service). You copy, paste into Plunk's editor, lose your formatting, redo the formatting, send. Plunk-It exists to remove that last mile.

This is the retrospective for the day Plunk-It first delivered on that promise — three commands operating against a real Plunk workspace, sending real campaigns.

## What's New?

(All historical, captured retrospectively. The arc: 2025-07-19 → 2025-08-15.)

- **A long pre-history.** Plunk-It's repo carries the shared cite-wide-lineage early commits — citation modal work that predates Plunk-It's identity by months. The repo was repurposed; the early commits aren't really Plunk-It's.
- **`refactor(main, services): refactor main and services to be a more generic starter kit. remove legacy citations code`** (2025-07-19) — the cleanup pass. Removed all the citation-modal scaffolding so the generic structure could become Plunk-It-specific.
- **`milestone(working-demo) Working demo with Send Email, Create Campaign, Send Campaign using Plunk`** (2025-08-15) — the first day Plunk-It actually did Plunk things. Three commands, all hitting a real Plunk workspace, all working.
- A burst of follow-on work over the next 48 hours: `Update Campaign command`, `working backlinks`, README work — the plugin moving from "a demo works" to "the demo works *and* the documentation explains it."

## The arc

Plunk-It is the most repurposed repo in the content-farm. It started life forked from cite-wide and spent most of July 2025 as an alternate citation-modal experiment before the team realized two things were true at once:

1. The citation work was better off concentrated in cite-wide and perplexed.
2. There was a separate, neglected need — markdown-to-email — that nothing in the farm addressed yet.

The July 19 refactor commit is the moment those realizations turned into action. "Refactor main and services to be a more generic starter kit. Remove legacy citations code" — straightforward, decisive. The repo became a blank canvas for a different problem.

The Plunk integration itself was straightforward once the canvas was clear. Plunk's API is well-documented and JSON-clean. The August 15 working demo was the result of about a week of focused work after the refactor.

## What this enabled

Plunk-It as it exists today is essentially the August 15 demo, plus polish:

- Configurable contact metadata (added 2025-08-18) instead of the hardcoded 'client' that the original demo used.
- Backlinks awareness (the "working backlinks" milestone) so the plugin can render link relationships when generating campaign content.
- Settings tab with full Plunk workspace configuration.

The "send your draft as an email straight from the editor" pitch on the content-farm splash is the August 15 demo, productionized.
